### New
- **Zoom on Export**: pinch or double-tap to zoom up to 3x and drag to pan. Before and After keep the same zoom, so you can compare the exact same spot. The magnifier button zooms back out.
- **Small-face hint**: when a hidden face is small in the preview, a hint suggests zooming in to check it.
- TalkBack: Zoom in and Zoom out to fit actions on the preview.

### Install or update
On Android 8.0 or newer, download **samar-1.3.apk** below and open it. If you have 1.2, samar offers this update itself; it keeps your Recent list.
